## Series A Pitch Deck | Oct 2026

## Slide 1: The Problem
### "We are drowning in information, but starving for wisdom."

1.  **The Forgetting Curve**: Humans forget 50% of what they learn within 1 hour, and 70% within 24 hours.
2.  **Passive Tooling**: Tools like **Notion** and **Google Docs** are "Digital Storage Lockers." You put files in, and they die there. They are static, disconnected, and dumb.
3.  **The EdTech Failure**: LMS platforms (Canvas, Blackboard) handle *logistics* (grades, attendance), not *cognition* (learning, retention).

**The Result:** Billions spent on education, with a <10% long-term retention rate.

---

## Slide 2: The Solution
### MindOS: Active Cognition Software

MindOS is not just a place to take notes. It is a **Second Brain that thinks with you.**

*   **Dynamic Knowledge Graphs**: Every concept is a node. Connect ideas visually, not just linearly.
*   **Integrated Spaced Repetition**: The system knows when you're about to forget and schedules reviews automatically.
*   **AI Neural Tutor**: A hyper-personalized tutor that "lives" in your graph, generating quizzes, mindmaps, and code simulations on demand.

**We don't just store information. We wire it into your long-term memory.**
